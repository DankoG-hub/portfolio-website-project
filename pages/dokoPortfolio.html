//01 — PROJECT OVERVIEW_

A clear introduction to a construction business.

DOKO Građenje is a construction company based in Croatia. The website presents its expertise, completed work and company information for prospective clients and project partners.

The experience supports Croatian-speaking visitors as well as people who prefer to browse in English. Its purpose is to help visitors understand the company, explore relevant projects and find a straightforward route to an enquiry.

//02 — THE CHALLENGE_

Let the construction work speak clearly.

A construction website needs to do more than introduce a business. Prospective clients need a useful way to assess its work, understand its capabilities and decide whether to start a conversation.

The owner came with a clear vision for colours, fonts and the overall visual appearance. A central priority was a strong portfolio section that could showcase construction projects from start to finish. My role was to turn those preferences and ideas into a coherent, usable website.

The strategic focus: connect project evidence with a clear next step, so visitors can move from browsing completed work to discussing their own requirements.

//03 — THE SOLUTION_

Explore the company. See the work. Get in touch.

I worked with the owner to bring the visual direction, content and project portfolio together through full web design and development. We then decided to make the website bilingual, supporting Croatian and English visitors. I also added and trained the chatbot on the company’s work-related content, connecting that information with the wider website experience.

  1. 01 / Establish context

    Company information introduces the business and its approach before visitors explore the work in more detail.

  2. 02 / Present the projects

    A browsable gallery leads to individual project pages, giving each piece of construction work its own space.

  3. 03 / Make contact simple

    Enquiry links, contact details and the AI assistant offer practical ways to find information and take the next step.

//04 — KEY FEATURES_

Useful details for a considered decision.

Croatian & English

Clearly labelled language options support local visitors and an English-speaking audience researching construction work in Croatia.

Individual project presentations

Dedicated project pages give visitors a closer look at the company’s work, beyond the overview offered by the main gallery.

Trained AI chatbot

I added and trained the Doko Assistant on the company’s work-related content. Quick options help visitors explore services, projects, location, opening hours and enquiries.

A straightforward user journey

Recognisable navigation, grouped company information and visible enquiry routes help visitors move between learning, exploring and making contact.

//05 — OUTCOME_

A presentable home for the company’s work.

Completed in 2025, the website gives DOKO Građenje a bilingual platform for presenting construction projects and company expertise, supported by direct enquiry routes and an AI information assistant.

It provides a foundation for future project updates and conversations with prospective clients. The value is in bringing the company’s work and practical visitor needs together in one coherent experience.
