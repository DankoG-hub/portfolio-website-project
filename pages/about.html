//Command Access Granted_

My work starts with a simple question: what does someone need to do, and how can I make that easier? That guides how I organise information, shape an interface, and choose the technology behind it.

Working independently means staying close to the whole process, from early ideas to the details of the finished experience. Alongside client projects, I’m exploring SaaS concepts and product prototypes, with an interest in how conversational AI can make everyday interactions more useful and intuitive.

//Personal Log _

Before working with websites, I spent years playing traditional Croatian music and guiding visitors along Croatia’s coast. Both involved connecting with people, paying attention, and finding a way to make an experience meaningful.

Moving to Dublin in 2019 opened a new chapter. Alongside my work as a multimedia and content analyst, I began developing my skills in design and web development. Goluza Interface Studio grew from that process: learning, making things, and taking responsibility for real projects.

The science-fiction influence you see here is personal. I’m drawn to interfaces that feel like part of a larger world, where small details invite you to look closer. This portfolio gives me space to explore that interest and develop a visual language of my own.

I’m still shaping where the studio goes next. What keeps me interested is the process itself: understanding an idea, figuring out how to build it, and seeing someone use what I’ve made.

//MISSION_

My mission is to help businesses communicate clearly and serve their customers through thoughtful web design and practical AI integrations.

The Next Horizon_

My vision is to grow Goluza Interface Studio into an independent digital product studio, building on client work to develop useful tools and services of its own.
